# Explicit cancellation of an unissued ordinary handoff

2026-11-07. This completes the abandonment path for a prepared ordinary-profile handoff. Host contracts are recorded in console `docs/reviews/pending-plan-operations-20261107.md`; the subsequent actual system gates are recorded in `docs/reviews/handoff-cancel-system-20261107.md`. This is not a recovery override, a service operation, or full Kura business acceptance.

Protocol-major 1 appends operation **29** `recovery.ordinary.cancel`. It reuses the strict field-13 `ordinaryHandoffAction={transactionId,confirmationToken}`; all previous operation/field numbers remain unchanged. Only the canonical `handoff-UUID` and its original confirmation UUID are accepted. There is no caller unit, path, command, snapshot or desired policy.

The existing explicitly enabled `AGENT_RECOVERY_ORDINARY_REMOTE=1` authority remains required. A current agent additionally advertises `recovery.ordinary.cancel.v1`; older `recovery.ordinary.v1` agents do not imply cancellation support. The center requires both capabilities, admin, tenant/ownership, the initial-password gate, CSRF, a fresh online connection and the current binding/connection epoch. Dispatch rechecks both capabilities. An unleased cancellation from a previous epoch expires; an issued uncertain cancellation is queried as its original command. Its TTL is 30 seconds. Other UNKNOWN/RUNNING writes still block cancellation. A duplicate admission returns the original task and never runs cancellation again.

A local administrator may call `recovery.ordinary.cancel` over the administrative UDS with exactly `{transactionId,confirmToken}`. A registered Kura connection cannot gain this method even with the same UID. Cancellation does not create an agent dependency for ordinary Kura, change the independent remote channel decision, or install/enable any feature by default.

Only `ORDINARY_PREPARED` with `issued=false` can be abandoned. The exact original token and immutable parent/grant/command evidence must match. Expiry revokes execution authority; it does not cause an unissued plan to stop the healthy parent. Background recovery validates the original preflight without requiring an unexpired approval and leaves it prepared for explicit cancellation. Other changed or unproven preflight facts retain their existing recovery-required behavior.

Cancellation reads archived evidence rather than invoking systemctl, live providers or Java. It never stops, starts, restarts or enables a unit, writes a snapshot, rolls back a completion, or reuses a recovery request. `RECOVERY_REQUIRED`, an issued intent, a handed-off unit or unrelated unresolved work cannot be cleared by cancellation.

The native durable sequence is `ORDINARY_PREPARED → CANCELLING_ORDINARY → ORDINARY_CANCELLED`. The intent precedes the create-only command decision and `.ordinary-cancel.json` receipt. The final journal links their exact digests. New optional Plan fields `cancelled` and `cancel_command` are omitted for legacy plans, preserving old receipt bodies. At most two decisions remain allowed: prepare plus execute, or prepare plus cancel. Original prepare decisions are checked against their full saved review result, including token and expiry.

On restart, an interrupted cancellation may finish only from its exact, already published receipt and linked decision. Receipt/file-directory durability is established before committing the terminal marker. Missing or changed evidence yields RR, preserves the scene and blocks writes; recovery never repeats cancellation or stops the healthy parent to compensate for an uncertain non-action. A local response lost after a changed native decision reports OUTCOME_UNKNOWN. A pure task query never repairs or advances native state.

The cancellation result is `state=ORDINARY_CANCELLED`, `cancelled=true`, `issued=false`, `ordinaryUnitHandedOff=false`, scope `administrator-selected-checks`, `normalBootVerified=false`, `kuraRecoveryVerified=false`. It proves abandonment, not new business checks. The original approved completion stays recorded. A later prepare validates and archives the exact cancelled journal create-only, then prepares from the retained original approved parent. Historical prepare/cancel tasks remain independently provable.

The existing original-command resolver now covers 21, 22 and 29. It authenticates the complete original protobuf request hash, original command/key/TTL/action and the linked private decision, source, grant and committed cancellation receipt. Missing, unlinked, changed or legacy evidence stays UNKNOWN. SQLite resolution and the center `task.reconcile.ordinary_handoff` audit occur once. Queries do not call systemctl, providers, replay, repair or policy reconciliation.

Agent and both Core maintainer scripts validate the same pure private cancellation evidence before accepting the new terminal state. Damaged source/grant/receipt, unsafe permissions, unlinked decisions or a changed original prepare token keep maintenance blocked. Older guards reject the new terminal state; upgrade the guard implementation before opting into this feature. Never delete a journal to force downgrade or package maintenance.

The bilingual panel exposes cancellation only with the new capability. It can cancel an expired unissued plan without execution approval, retains only intent/key/task IDs in browser storage, and obtains the original ID after a lost acknowledgement. UNKNOWN and a 404 intent keep writes gated; no replacement cancellation or handoff is automatically created. Issued/full-recovery overclaims are rejected by the center and UI.

The host contracts are followed by fresh full IDEA Kura/original GWT and actual OrbStack and existing Parallels validation, recorded in console `docs/reviews/handoff-cancel-system-20261107.md`. Each Linux fixture established a new real approved Kura parent, cancelled two distinct unissued plans through the bound center, retained original decisions/history and left the parent PID, invocation, snapshots and selected DS business unchanged. Natural expiry followed by agent reopen preserved the prepared parent; Parallels also proved actual CapEff=0, NoNewPrivs=1 and EROFS. These direct-success runs do not claim a new op29 UNKNOWN fault injection or full Kura recovery. Current review artifact is handoffcancel1; prior systemcontrol3 lacks operation29. Core package installation/upgrade, other unresolved operations, full component business coverage and formal release remain separate deliveries. All new fixture processes and VMs were stopped with evidence retained; original Orb services/packages were preserved.
